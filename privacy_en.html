🔒 Privacy Policy

Color Cage

Color Cage (hereinafter "the App") values user privacy and complies with applicable privacy laws. The App can be used without registration, and game progress is stored only on the user's device.

1. Information Collection

The App does not collect or store personal information (name, phone, email, etc.) through any developer-operated server. However, third-party services (Google AdMob) integrated in the App may collect the following data for advertising purposes:

  • Advertising data, device identifiers (IDFA), app usage data, diagnostic information

2. Purpose of Data Use

  • Advertising: Providing personalized or general ads (Google AdMob)
  • App Service: Storing game data (levels, scores) locally on device

3. Third-Party Data Sharing

  • Recipient: Google (AdMob)
  • Purpose: Personalized advertising and ad performance analysis
  • Related Policy: Google Privacy Policy
